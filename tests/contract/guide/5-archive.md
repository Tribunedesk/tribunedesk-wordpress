# Level 5 – Archive

Not available yet, and no date is promised. If your newsroom needs it, ask your Tribune Desk contact.

Today older articles become link targets only through the link back, and only those Tribune Desk wrote. Level 5 would let your CMS send its older articles (title, text, address, date), so new articles could link to them and show them in “read more” boxes. It gets its own chapter if it is built.
